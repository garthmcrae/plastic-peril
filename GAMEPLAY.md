# Gameplay

You play against your child. You print the map. You are the game master. The child enters the dungeon armed with one d6, three life boxes, a miniature of their choice, and a fistful of luck.

The adult reads the story, ticks off life boxes, rolls the Enemy and Events d6s, and quietly steers the game. The child rolls everything else. Rules are flexible. Borderline cheating to keep it interesting is fine — it is the job.

## Setup

1. Print the sheet — the map already has obstacles on it.
2. Child picks toys/miniatures and places one on **every obstacle tile**. Those minis are the encounters. Bad guys, monsters, anyone who has wronged the household pet. Their choice.
3. Child puts their own mini on the entrance tile.
4. Adult reads the **introduction** out loud.

## How squares work

- The kid moves orthogonally — up, down, left, right.
- **Obstacle tiles are not enterable.** A toy sits on each one. You cannot step onto a square that already has a mini on it.
- An **encounter** triggers when the kid's mini moves onto a square **adjacent to** an obstacle's mini. Fight from one square away. The mini stays on its tile until the fight resolves.

## Turn loop

**1. Kid rolls the d6 and moves.**
- Move up to that many squares, or stop when you arrive adjacent to an obstacle/mini (encounter).
- If you stop next to an enemy, fight (step 2).
- If you didn't end up next to anything, no fight this turn — go straight to step 3.

**2. Fight (only if adjacent to an enemy mini). Sequential rolls.**
- Child rolls the **Combat d6** on the sheet. Adult reads that result out loud and narrates it.
- Then the adult rolls the **Enemy d6** in response. Read it as the enemy's reaction to what the kid just did — one beat, not two.
- Combat slot 6 is always a **kill** → the enemy goes up in a puff of smoke, remove the mini, fight ends. Skip the Enemy roll.
- Other Combat slots may **resolve** the fight too — pacified, befriended (see Companions), or fight pauses. If resolved, skip the Enemy roll.
- If the Enemy d6 lands on a wound result, tick a life box.
- If neither side ended it, the fight continues next turn — kid rolls to move again (stay adjacent) and re-rolls Combat.

**Spells.** The **Spells d6** is a GM toolbox, not a fixed slot. Pull from it when the fiction asks for magic — a Combat or Enemy result that mentions a spell, an Events roll that sparks one, or just because the kid said "I cast a spell". Roll the Spells d6, read the result, apply it.

**3. Events — every turn, no exceptions.**
- After the move (and after any fight rolls), adult rolls the **Events d6**.
- Something happens to the map: rumbles, drips, falling rocks, whispering walls.
- Read the slot out loud. Apply any effect (lose a move, take a wound, heal a life, move an enemy).
- Events fire whether the kid encountered an enemy or just walked across an empty corridor — the dungeon is always doing something.

## Companions

Some Combat and Spells results turn an enemy into a **companion** — pacified, charmed, befriended. When that happens:

- The companion's mini is no longer an obstacle. Pick it up off its tile.
- It moves with the kid. Drop it on the kid's tile (or right beside it) and drag it along on every move.
- Companions do not take wounds and do not die. They are not removed by Combat 6.
- **In a fight, the kid rolls Combat once per companion, plus once for themselves.** One companion = 2 rolls. Two companions = 3 rolls. Each roll resolves independently — any of them can kill, pacify, charm, or just whiff.
- The adult still rolls the Enemy d6 once in response to the whole flurry.

## Win and lose

**Win.** When the child's mini reaches the exit tile, the adult reads the **conclusion**.

**Lose.** Three wounds and the child is out. The adult is allowed to forget the last wound if the child's face is doing The Thing.

## Example playback

Setup. Dad unfolds the sheet — a 10×10 cave, jaggy walls, entrance up top, exit at the bottom-left, sidebar with Life, Spells, Combat, Enemy, Events, and a kind conclusion about a damp sock. Kid roots through the toy box. A chipped skeleton on the first obstacle. A pink goblin on the second. A faceless Lego knight on the third. Troll doll on the entrance tile.

> Dad: "You're in the Cave of the Lost Sock. Something in the dark is humming. Roll."

**Turn 1.** Kid rolls a 4. Counts squares — one, two — and stops at the square next to the skeleton on tile three. Encounter.

- Combat d6: 2. *You trip on your own boot, land on the enemy — you take a wound.* Dad ticks a life box.
- Enemy d6: 1. *Skeleton poos pants — falls over backwards, no attack.* Reacting to the kid landing on it.
- Events d6: 2. *Distant rumble — the cave groans, dust falls.* Nobody hurt, but everyone hears it.

**Turn 2.** Kid rolls a 5, stays adjacent to the skeleton.

- Combat d6: 3. *Solid whack — skeleton is stunned, joins you as a companion.* Dad picks the skeleton up off its tile and drops it next to the kid's mini. Fight ends, no Enemy roll.
- Events d6: 5. *A small rock pings off your head — take a wound.* Dad ticks a life box. Kid scowls.

**Turn 3.** Kid rolls a 6 and walks past the empty tile, lands adjacent to the goblin. Encounter — kid has one companion, so rolls Combat **twice**.

- Combat d6 (kid): 6. *Critical hit — goblin puffs into smoke, mini removed.* Fight ends after one roll — the second Combat roll isn't needed because there's no enemy left.
- Events d6: 3. *A bat flaps past your face — flinch, lose your next move.* Dad pretends to be a bat.

**Turn 4.** No move (bat). Events d6: 1. *A wet drip in the dark — nothing else happens.* Free turn.

**Turn 5.** Kid rolls a 4, lands next to the knight. One companion — two Combat rolls.

- Combat d6 (kid): 4. *You offer it your snack — knight is charmed, joins you as a companion.* Fight ends, second roll skipped, no Enemy roll.
- Events d6: 6. *Boulder thuds in the next chamber — GM moves any one enemy one square.* No enemies left on the map, so nothing to move. Dad lets it slide.

**Turn 6.** Kid rolls a 5, walks the rest of the way to the exit with a skeleton and a knight in tow.

Dad reads the conclusion out loud. Damp sock. Kid laughs. Sheet goes on the fridge.

Total time: eleven minutes. Three rules bent. Two companions. One bat.
