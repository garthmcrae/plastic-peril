# Gameplay

You play against your child. You print the map. You are the game master. The child enters the dungeon armed with one d6, three life boxes, a miniature of their choice, and a fistful of luck.

The adult reads the story, ticks off life boxes, rolls the Enemy and Events d6s, and quietly steers the game. The child rolls everything else. Rules are flexible. Borderline cheating to keep it interesting is fine — it is the job.

## Setup

1. Print the sheet — the map already has obstacles on it.
2. Child picks toys/miniatures and places one on **every obstacle tile**. Those minis are the encounters. Bad guys, monsters, anyone who has wronged the household pet. Their choice.
3. Child puts their own mini on the entrance tile.
4. Adult reads the **narrative** opening paragraph out loud and notes the bracketed GM prompts.

## How squares work

- The kid moves orthogonally — up, down, left, right.
- **Obstacle tiles are not enterable.** A toy sits on each one. You cannot step onto a square that already has a mini on it.
- An **encounter** triggers when the kid's mini moves onto a square **adjacent to** an obstacle's mini. Fight from one square away. The mini stays on its tile until it's beaten.

## Turn loop

**1. Kid rolls the d6 and moves.**
- Move up to that many squares, or stop when you arrive adjacent to an obstacle/mini (encounter).
- If you stop next to an enemy, fight (step 2 + 3).
- If you didn't end up next to anything, no fight this turn — go straight to step 3.

**2. Fight (only if adjacent to an enemy mini).**
- Child rolls the **Combat d6** on the sheet.
- Adult rolls the **Enemy d6** on the sheet.
- Both effects happen in the same turn. Read them in order, narrate.
- Combat slot 4 is always **"cast a spell"** → child rolls the **Spells d6** instead.
- Combat slot 6 is always **a kill** → fight ends, remove the toy.
- If the Enemy d6 lands on a wound result, tick a life box.
- If neither side ended it, the fight continues next turn — kid rolls to move again (stay adjacent) and re-rolls combat.

**3. Events — every turn, no exceptions.**
- After the move (and after any fight rolls), adult rolls the **Events d6**.
- Something happens to the map: rumbles, drips, falling rocks, whispering walls.
- Read the slot out loud. Apply any effect (lose a move, take a wound, heal a life, move an enemy).
- Events fire whether the kid encountered an enemy or just walked across an empty corridor — the dungeon is always doing something.

**Win.** When the child's mini reaches the exit tile, the adult reads the **ending** line on the sheet.

**Lose.** Three wounds and the child is out. The adult is allowed to forget the last wound if the child's face is doing The Thing.

## Example playback

Setup. Dad unfolds the sheet — a 10×10 cave, jaggy walls, entrance up top, exit at the bottom-left, sidebar with Life, Spells, Combat, Enemy, Events, and a kind ending about a damp sock. Kid roots through the toy box. A chipped skeleton on the first obstacle. A pink goblin on the second. A faceless Lego knight on the third. Troll doll on the entrance tile.

> Dad: "You're in the Cave of the Lost Sock. Something in the dark is humming. Roll."

**Turn 1.** Kid rolls a 4. Counts squares — one, two — and stops at the square next to the skeleton on tile three. Encounter.

- Combat d6: 2. *You trip on your own boot, land on the enemy (wound).*
- Enemy d6: 1. *Poos pants — no attack.*
- Events d6: Dad rolls. 2. *Distant rumble — the cave groans, dust falls.* Nobody hurt, but everyone hears it.

**Turn 2.** Kid rolls a 5, stays adjacent to the skeleton to keep fighting.

- Combat d6: 4. *Cast a spell.* Spells d6: 3. *Stinkbreath.* Next enemy skips its roll.
- Enemy d6: 6. *Shrugs it off and glares.* Unbothered, downwind of stinkbreath.
- Events d6: 5. *A small rock pings off your head — take a wound.* Dad ticks a life box. Kid scowls.

**Turn 3.** Kid rolls a 6.

- Combat d6: 6. *Critical kill.* Skeleton removed.
- Kid uses remaining move to walk past the empty tile and around the corner, landing on an empty square — not adjacent to anything.
- Events d6: 3. *A bat flaps past your face — flinch, lose your next move.* Dad pretends to be a bat.

**Turn 4.** No fight (lost the move from the bat). Events d6: 1. *A wet drip in the dark — nothing else happens.* Free turn.

**Turn 5.** Kid rolls a 4, lands next to the goblin.

- Combat d6: 3. *Solid whack — enemy takes a wound.* (Goblin still up.)
- Enemy d6: 2. *Wild swing — you take a wound.* Dad ticks a life box.
- Events d6: 6. *Boulder thuds in the next chamber — GM moves any one enemy one square.* Dad shuffles the Lego knight one square closer. Kid groans.

**Turn 6.** Kid rolls, stays adjacent, Combat 6. Goblin done. Dad eyeballs the knight and the one remaining life box and quietly skips the Events roll this once.

**Turn 7.** Kid rolls a 5, slips past the knight on a technicality, steps on the exit.

Dad reads the ending out loud. Damp sock. Kid laughs. Sheet goes on the fridge.

Total time: thirteen minutes. Three rules bent. One stinkbreath. One bat.
