# Forbidden Bridge

Board game first released in 1992 by Milton Bradley. Players are explorers searching for ancient jewels guarded by an angry spirit. Retrieve two jewels and return them to your boat before opponents.

## Components

- Motorized bridge with 2 spans connecting an idol and cliff (13 playable spaces, 7 railings)
- Idol cliff with movable hands and platform
- Climbing cliff piece
- Gameboard with plastic rocks and optional jungle scenery slides
- Four explorer pawns and boats
- 15 jewels with storage bag
- Two dice (one numbered, one special)

## Gameplay

2–4 players, ages 7+. Each turn, roll both dice:

- **Numbered die** – values 2, 3, 5 (each repeated twice); move that many spaces.
- **Special die** – three outcomes (each on two sides):
  - **Idol icon** – The idol becomes angry. Press its head down to shake the bridge; any pieces that fall move to the closest land space (pieces cannot be in water).
  - **Explorer icon** – Move another player's piece to a different position on their bridge space. Position matters — some spots are safer when the bridge shakes.
  - **Jewel icon** – Steal another player's jewel if you are on the same space.

## Special Features

The motorized idol requires no batteries. When activated by pressing the head down, the mechanism winds up and releases to shake the bridge. The idol base displays five warning glyphs symbolizing the explorer's journey and the danger involved.

## 2021 Remake

Hasbro Gaming released an updated version in Q3 2021, distributed exclusively through Target.
