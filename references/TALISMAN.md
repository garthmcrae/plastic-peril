# Talisman

Fantasy adventure board game first released in 1983 by Games Workshop. Players are adventurers competing to reach the Crown of Command at the center of the board. Along the way they gain strength, magic, followers, weapons, and treasures while surviving monsters, traps, curses, and rival players.

## Components

- Three-region game board:
  - **Outer Region** – safer starting area with towns, forests, plains, and basic encounters
  - **Middle Region** – more dangerous spaces requiring stronger characters
  - **Inner Region** – deadly final path leading to the Crown of Command
- Character cards with unique abilities and starting attributes
- Adventure cards containing enemies, events, strangers, objects, followers, and places
- Spell cards used by magical characters
- Gold, life, strength, and craft counters
- Toad cards for transformed players
- Alignment system:
  - Good
  - Neutral
  - Evil
- Six-sided dice

## Gameplay

2–6 players depending on edition. Each player chooses a character such as a wizard, warrior, thief, dwarf, troll, or prophetess.

### Turn Structure

On a turn:

1. Roll one die for movement.
2. Move clockwise or counterclockwise depending on region rules.
3. Resolve the space landed on:
   - Draw Adventure cards
   - Fight enemies
   - Visit shops or strangers
   - Gain followers or objects
   - Encounter other players

### Combat

Two major attributes determine combat:

- **Strength** – used for physical combat
- **Craft** – used for psychic or magical combat

Combat is generally resolved by:

- Rolling one die
- Adding Strength or Craft
- Comparing totals with the opponent

Losers typically lose one life.

## Progression

Characters become stronger over time by:

- Defeating enemies
- Collecting gold and equipment
- Gaining followers
- Increasing Strength and Craft
- Learning spells

Players may also suffer setbacks including:

- Losing items
- Being turned into a toad
- Death and restarting
- Alignment restrictions
- Random disasters

## Winning the Game

To win, a player must:

1. Reach the Crown of Command in the Inner Region.
2. Survive the final sequence of dangerous spaces.
3. Claim the Crown of Command.
4. Eliminate or outlast all other players.

In many editions, the Crown allows the winner to repeatedly cast a command spell damaging all remaining players until only one survives.

## Special Features

- Highly random, story-driven gameplay focused on emergent adventures rather than strict balance
- Characters vary dramatically in power and play style
- Many spaces permanently change during play as cards accumulate on the board
- Direct player conflict is encouraged
- Strong fantasy themes inspired by classic sword-and-sorcery fiction

## Expansions

Talisman received many expansions across multiple editions, adding:

- New regions and side boards
- Additional characters
- More spells and enemies
- Alternative endings
- New mechanics such as dungeons, highlands, dragons, and city adventures

Notable expansions include:

- The Dungeon
- The City
- The Highlands
- The Dragon
- The Reaper

## Fourth Edition Revised

Fantasy Flight Games released a revised Fourth Edition in 2007 with updated artwork, streamlined rules, plastic miniatures, and extensive expansion support. This version became the modern standard edition for many players.
